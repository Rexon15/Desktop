Did you know that there are more than 8.3 billion people on earth

Humans (Homo sapiens) are the only living species in the genus Homo, having first emerged in Africa about 300,000 years ago. Today, the global population has surpassed 8.3 billion people, spanning all continents and driving major shifts in the planet's ecosystems and technology.

Humans are the only mammals that cannot breathe and swallow at the same time.

Humans are the only species known to shed emotional tears.
The human brain generates enough electricity to power a small LED light bulb.

Humans (homo sapiens)are uniquely built for extreme long-distance running, a trait that allowed our ancestors to hunt down faster prey through persistence. Unlike most mammals, which rely on panting and must stop to cool down, humans possess millions of sweat glands and minimal body hair, allowing us to shed heat efficiently while moving. Combined with our upright posture, spring-like foot arches, and large gluteal muscles, this specialized cooling system allowed early humans to chase animals for hours in the midday heat until the prey collapsed from exhaustion.

Humans are the only known creatures in theworld universe to have developed a system of writing to record their own thoughts andhistory.
