Rocket Mission Gallery

Rockets are vehicles that are propelled by the expulsion of gases from a rocket engine. They are used for space exploration, satellite launches, and military applications. Rockets work on the principle of Newton's third law of motion, which states that for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. When the rocket engine burns fuel, it produces hot gases that are expelled out of the back of the rocket, propelling it forward.
