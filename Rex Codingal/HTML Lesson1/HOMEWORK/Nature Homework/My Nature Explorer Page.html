Look closely, explore outside, and discover something new!

Welcome, Explorer!

Nature is all around us. You can explore a garden, a park, or even the plants near your home.

What can you discover?

Plants 🌱

Look for leaves, flowers, and trees. Do the leaves have different shapes or colors?

Animals 🐦

Watch for birds, butterflies, or insects. Look quietly so you do not scare them.

Weather ☀️

Notice if the day is sunny, cloudy, windy, or rainy. How does the weather feel?

My Explorer Checklist

  • I spotted a plant.
  • I listened for an animal.
  • I noticed the weather.
Remember: Stay with a grown-up, be gentle with living things, and leave nature as you found it.
