My Fitness Routine

A simple full-body workout to build strength and energy.

20 minDuration
EasyLevel
3xPer week

Workout Plan

  • Warm-up: March in place for 3 minutes
  • Squats: 3 sets of 10
  • Push-ups: 3 sets of 8
  • Jumping jacks: 3 sets of 20
  • Cool-down: Stretch for 5 minutes
Fitness tip

Drink water, focus on good form, and rest whenever you need to.
