Your Mini Adventure

Try these three simple challenges. Complete them at your own pace and have fun!

🌳 Nature Spotter

Find three different leaves near the trail.

🎯 Target Toss

Toss a soft beanbag into a basket from a short distance.

🧩 Trail Riddle

Answer one easy nature riddle at the park sign.

Before You Start

  • Stay with your grown-up or group.
  • Walk on the marked paths.
  • Take turns and cheer for everyone.
  • Drink water and take a break whenever you need one.

Every explorer is a winner. Have an awesome adventure!
